- Two anonymized selections by ascending compressed member size: index 0 and index 54, with SHA256:
  - index 0: `48becf1963ffe80f3018cf2f74a3243e3c84b0e8ac5af30b53323bb2b310dd9b`
  - index 54: `4efedb0a58ee0d01d264bed342f0c87536f5f8db5b70c705ddc3d894dfde5925`
- All three model shapes v2.4/v3.3/v3.5 were evaluated against a single v2.4 RAS-initialized rigid ICP transform per CT example, with fixed calibration and no model scale optimization.

- Both masks show positive segmentation voxels at their original crop border, so **complete anatomy is NOT verified**. Zero-padding produced a numerically closed surface, potentially adding an artificial crop-edge cap.

## Numeric label mapping
Each selected source mask has seven positive integer classes 1,2,3,4,5,6,7. **No official numeric-ID-to-anatomical-substructure mapping was verified. Do not name any class 'vertebral body', 'lamina', 'articular process', etc.**

## Lessons and next action
1. Two real L3-labeled masks (not just whole-L3 masks) were read and analyzed with numeric label identifiers. Tests completed on both examples.
2. Shape correction v3.5 is **not a consistent improvement across examples**; do not promote or publish it. Cases with opposite effects must be investigated.
3. Numeric class 5 has large residual on both examples, but its true anatomy is UNCONFIRMED. Do not modify 'part 5' by guessed region label.
4. A crop boundary may cause fake caps and bias the error metric; quantify crop-edge involvement and prioritize complete L3 subregions for future comparisons.
5. Source-exact label legend, orientation/expert registration, and multiple representative samples are essential before inferring true anatomical quality.
6. Original v2.4 remains the only public shape. Existing v3.3/v3.5 are immutable research candidates.
