# L3 root-cause and corrective audit v3.9–v4.5
Date: 2026-11-02 (JST). **Research/quality engineering. NOT anatomical teaching approval.**

## 1. Critical verified diagnosis
Previously the pipeline initialized every CT-to-model comparison with the same image coordinate pose. The LumASe L3 NIfTI source masks have **different header axis codes**:
- anonymized size-sorted mask index 0: **RAS**, qform_code=1, sform_code=0.
- index 54: **LPS**, qform_code=0, sform_code=2.
- index 65: **LPS**, qform_code=0, sform_code=2.
- index 87: **LPS**, qform_code=0, sform_code=2.

The NIfTI header information is objective; the **independent anatomy/model axis correspondence remains unverified**. These are not patient IDs.

## 2. Four proper rotation initializations tested on the v2.4 baseline
A fixed set of 0-degree nominal / 180-degree rotations around X, Y, Z (all determinant +1) was fitted with rigid ICP. The scoring target was sampled symmetric surface RMS, not an anatomically identified matching set.

| Anonymous case index | lowest sampled RMS hypothesis | nominal RMS (mm) | lowest RMS (mm) | score margin to runner-up |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | yaw 180° about Z | 6.266 | 4.515 | 3.24% |
| 54 | nominal identity | 4.058 | 4.058 | 3.68% |
| 65 | nominal identity | 4.537 | 4.537 | ~3.46% |
| 87 | nominal identity | 4.570 | 4.570 | ~2.89% |

**All cases have <5% score gaps**, so the provisional best-scoring pose is **ambiguous by the research program's heuristic**. Do not automatically designate the inferred orientation as anatomical truth. We still lack a human-reviewed landmark match.

## 3. Causal effect of correcting the single-orientation assumption
Example index 0:
- nominal RAS-initialized ICP: baseline CT->model P95=**16.640 mm**, candidate v3.5 P95=**16.618 mm** (improvement 0.022 mm).
- yaw-180-initialized ICP: baseline P95=**14.541 mm**, candidate P95=**11.810 mm** (improvement **2.731 mm**).
- Under yaw-180 the numeric class 5 centroid changes from approx Y=+40.97mm to Y=-34.40mm in model coordinates.
- A difference in orientation assumption therefore **materially changed the apparent effect** of the SAME geometry edit.
- Example 54 stayed closest to nominal pose: baseline **12.827 mm**, v3.5 **10.376 mm**.

This supports a measurement/registration-root-cause **independent of any unproven claim that the original L3 mesh is anatomically correct**.

## 4. Exhaustive source crop/label data review
- Public dataset: LumASe, Zenodo record 7181338, CC BY 4.0, ZIP checksum metadata md5:baf6374a927e9591dbae3314570bdb1e.
- **All 108** files matched the archive's L3 mask filename convention and had positive numeric labels **1–7**.
- **All 108** showed some boundary contact. All had total six-face boundary-voxel fraction <0.5% of occupied mask voxels.
- Thus 'mask touches image edge' does **not** prove a large anatomical truncation. However, artificial caps from padding must not contaminate shape distance metrics.
- v4.0 excluded triangle centroids within 2 voxels of each image border and exterior label voxels near borders.
- 4 controlled CT cases (0,54,65,87) all passed; 1.13%–4.72% of sampled exterior mesh triangles were removed by the filter in these cases.
- Numeric class 5 baseline P95 after filtering remained large in all four cases (~20–22 mm), so the original residual is not explained entirely by artificial crop caps.
- These results are *point-sampled proxy distances*, not independently validated region geometry.

## 7. Unresolved blocking issues and next steps
1. Numeric label **1–7 anatomy names are UNVERIFIED** from the original LumASe publication; do not infer names from size/location, harmonized derivative datasets or model assumptions.
2. Anatomical landmark orientation confirmation must override low-level surface RMS; two almost-equivalent rigid rotations can differ meaningfully anatomically.
3. No matching named region labels in the independent author-created model. CT label->whole-model surface proximity is not equivalent to verifying facet/lamina morphology.
4. High residual errors remain; v3.5 is not anatomically approved, and cannot yet be promoted as a finished L3.
5. Further CT cases should be held out from parameter tuning; avoid overfitting two or four research examples. Document acceptance tolerances and systematic reference variation before anatomical pass.
6. Keep focus on L3 until true structural correspondence, registered normals, and morphology meet independent review.
