# L3 v4.7: Four held-out CT reference cases / bidirectional quality audit

Date: 2026-11-03 (JST). **Research only; anatomy/teaching approval NOT established.**

## Purpose
Determine whether experimental v3.5 improvement over v2.4 generalizes outside the cases used during shape design and whether a longer posterior process worsens model-to-reference distance (over-extension). No reference mask or subject CT was committed to the repository.

## Sources and scope
- Public LumASe dataset, Zenodo record 7181338, CC BY 4.0, archive md5 `baf6374a927e9591dbae3314570bdb1e`.
- Four anonymously indexed L3 masks **20, 48, 83, 102**, selected from the source inventory by low border-contact metadata, not by 3D shape score. Previously inventoried/crop-screened, but **not used for shape parameter tuning** (not an independently blinded test of clinical validity).
- Per-mask SHA256 verified against prior archive audit; archive count 108 L3 entries verified.
- Compared original v2.4 with experimental v3.3 and v3.5, at fixed v2.4 CT/model alignment and no scale matching.
- Registration attempts identity and ±20° initial rotations around each principal axis and limits *final* rotation to 35° from the assumed RAS-consistent authored pose. This assumed anatomy prior was **not independently validated**.
- For each model, sampled 5,000 points on the CT surface and 5,000 on the model surface using deterministic fixed seeds. CT crop-border artificial caps excluded from the reference surface.
- P95 surface distances below are point-cloud nearest-neighbor **proxy** metrics; not same-region anatomy comparison, not CT calibrated clinical reconstruction, and not robust uncertainty intervals.

### Observed pattern
- v3.5 improved bidirectional and CT -> model P95 **in all four selected holdout examples**.
- Model -> CT P95 worsened in 20 (+0.357 mm) and 83 (+0.073 mm). These changes are not proven to be outside resampling variability, and are not evidence of clinical acceptability.
- In all four examples, at least one 180° *pitch* inversion was within 5% of the small-angle pose fit score. **Orientation is ambiguous by this heuristic, not anatomically validated**, so these errors cannot support definitive shape promotion.
- All selected examples have positive segmentation at crop borders, which is filtered, but completeness cannot be proven.
- Numeric classes 1–7 are present, but no official numeric-to-anatomical label mapping was independently verified; do not call any numeric label vertebral body/lamina/facet.

## Root cause assessment
1. **Supported provisional morphology hypothesis**: current hand-coded posterior elements in v2.4 do not cover CT-derived surface consistently; adding posterior spinous and inferior elements increases coverage across four unseen geometry comparison cases.
2. **Unresolved verification barrier**: CT orientation to independent anatomical landmarks, authoritative mapping of 1–7, matching model substructure labels, uncertainty due to surface sampling/voxel resolution.
3. **Scientific integrity**: The 35° limit is a hypothesis, **not** a verified anatomical registration. Never use a lowest-error point-cloud fit to claim human anatomy or surgical accuracy.
4. **Acceptance decision**: KEEP v3.5 as research candidate; DO NOT overwrite public v2.4, DO NOT approve anatomy for teaching. Next investigate repeatability across different random surface samples and source-derived landmarks with explicit human verification.
