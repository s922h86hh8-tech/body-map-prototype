# L3 v4.6: world-axis-constrained CT registration (technical audit)
Date: 2026-11-03 (JST). **Research only; NO anatomy teaching approval.**

## The cause being corrected
Earlier v4.3/v4.5 chose the lowest-surface-distance of four rigid orientations, sometimes a 180-degree rotation. Rigid surface similarity without anatomical landmarks is insufficient to certify front/back and superior/inferior orientation.
NIfTI voxel axis codes vary between source mask entries (RAS or LPS); after applying the NIfTI affine, coordinates are both in RAS world convention. The authored geometry is assumed +X right, +Y anterior, +Z superior. This assumption is NOT independently expert-verified.

## v4.6 design and controls
- Candidate alignment starts: identity and ±20 degrees around each of X/Y/Z; max final rotation from identity 35 degrees.
- 180-degree yaw, pitch, and roll are still evaluated as **negative controls**, not candidates for automatic anatomical selection.
- Fixed v2.4 registration is applied unchanged to v3.3 and v3.5. CT masks are from the public LumASe record 7181338; numeric classes 1–7 still have **no source-verified anatomical names**.
- All compared samples exclude the two-voxel reference crop edge where surfaces can be artificially closed.
- Real CT masks stay temporary in GitHub Actions; no subject data or raw references committed.

This **does not** prove the 180° model is more anatomically correct, nor does the axis prior prove the under-35° fit is correct. It exposes an unresolved anatomical registration question and possible model-shape asymmetry.

## Decision and explicit blockers
1. **Technical orientation guard passes synthetic test**. Four real-mask jobs completed successfully.
2. Real data pose under the prior is provisional; no independent anatomical landmark validation. Numeric class-to-anatomical mapping is still unverified.
3. v3.5 correction reduced CT-to-model P95 in indices 54, 65 and 87 under restricted-axis registration but not index 0; this heterogeneity and orientation uncertainty prevent model promotion.
4. No anatomical/teaching approval. Keep published v2.4, experiment v3.3/v3.5 unchanged.
5. Next actual cause-removal action: establish a source-backed anatomical coordinate anchor/label map on real CT, then compute *same-region* errors. Do not invent numeric label mappings.
