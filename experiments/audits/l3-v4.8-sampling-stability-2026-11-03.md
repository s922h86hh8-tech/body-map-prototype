# L3 v4.8 — Fixed-pose surface sampling variability (four untouched case indices)

Date: 2026-11-03 JST. **Not anatomical approval.**

## Method
The five runs within each CT case use the SAME baseline registration (the v4.7 small-angle RAS-axis prior pose, not independent expert anatomy registration). Each repetition independently resamples the CT reference surface and both model surfaces with deterministic seeds. Five repeats × four held-out cases; 5,000 points per surface per repeat. No CT data or original images were retained by the GitHub Actions workflow. Cases selected by low crop-boundary contact metadata, not shape agreement. LumASe source DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7181338; CC BY 4.0.

## Root causes and next action
1. v3.5 *covers more of the CT surface* (improvement stable in all 20 trials) even with fixed registration. **Research morphology hypothesis supported**.
2. Case 20 consistently shows a *small model over-coverage or misplaced geometry warning* (+0.378 to +0.572 mm in model-to-CT P95). The exact part responsible is **not yet identified**; its cause may be sampling geometry, individual variation or the unverified registration.
3. In the other three cases, model-to-CT change varies around zero and cannot be designated a systematic improvement or failure.
4. NEXT: isolate model-to-CT error geographically for case 20 and compare v3.3 vs v3.5 at same orientation; test a smaller or localized inferior-process extension rather than arbitrary global changes. Keep the previously tuned cases separate from held-out tests; if case 20 is used for correction it ceases to be a holdout.
5. No independently verified numeric-label 1–7 anatomy mapping, no independently registered facet normals/CT landmarks and no reviewed educational geometry acceptance thresholds.
